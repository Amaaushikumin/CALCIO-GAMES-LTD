The studio

Calcio Games Ltd is a private, independent company based in London. “Calcio” is simply the Italian word for football, and it’s a fair summary of the brief.

We publish software for mobile and desktop, design our own games, and work with freelance artists and sound designers where it makes the game better. No investors to impress, which means we can take the time to get the small things right.

We design for short sessions, respect players’ time, and keep monetisation clear and fair.
